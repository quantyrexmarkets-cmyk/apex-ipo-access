TeraFab — Sovereign Silicon for the Musk Ecosystem.

Direct construction-phase capital participation in the $16.8B captive fab jointly funded by Tesla, SpaceX and Intel.

Implied Valuation$16.8B pre-money
StatusConstruction JV · Capital Raise Open
Access TypeDirect Build-Phase Participation

TeraFab is a captive joint venture between Tesla (TSLA), SpaceX (SPCX) and Intel (INTC) — pooling internal corporate resources to build a $16.8 billion advanced-node semiconductor facility in Grimes County, Texas. Unlike a conventional foundry startup, every wafer TeraFab produces is spoken for before it leaves the cleanroom, secured by binding offtake commitments from the two anchor customers.

The venture solves a strategic supply-chain vulnerability that has defined the last decade of Musk-ecosystem hardware: dependence on offshore foundries for Dojo AI training chips, Starlink phased-array radios, Optimus neural cores and radiation-hardened Starship avionics. First silicon is targeted for Q4 2028, with an anticipated public listing window in Q3 2029 – Q1 2030 once volume production is validated.
